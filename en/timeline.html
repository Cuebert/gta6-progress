18:28:30 UTCTiming review

GPU diagnostics accounted for much of the cutscene delay

On the same build and material settings, seven intervals with device-removal diagnostics enabled had a median of 8,066.5 ms per frame at 1,706 draws. These timings cover the Prologue cutscene.
