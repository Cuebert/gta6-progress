01:44:59 UTCBridge-water comparison

The opt-in Composite clamp preserved selected bridge glints

In separate runs of the same first-boat bridge scene, 585 of 1,839 selected high-HDR reflection texels in the stock Composite were near-black in packed RGB10. With the opt-in first-grading-lookup clamp, 0 of 1,826 selected texels were near-black and all 1,826 were bright in the Composite RGB10 output. At actual Present, the overlapping stock display region contained 992 dark samples and no bright samples; the clamp region contained 0 dark samples and 1,713 bright samples. High-HDR selection used red ≥1,000 with the recorded green/red and blue/red ranges; near-black means all RGB10 channels ≤32 and bright means all channels ≥896. The runs used the same bridge scene and normalized glint selector, but water waves and camera differed, so they are not pixel-registered frames. This verifies the selected bridge-glint path through Composite and Present in this comparison. Face, sky and clothing rendering remain open. Compare the water-only crops →
